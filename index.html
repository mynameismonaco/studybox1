A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Everything else you might be wondering.

What exactly is a StudyBox?+

StudyBox is a startup concept for private, sound-insulated study pods inside universities. Each pod is designed with a desk, comfortable seating, lighting and charging. This website demonstrates the proposed experience.

Can I make a real reservation?+

This is an interactive front-end demo. You can choose a campus, reserve a simulated pod and try the QR unlock flow. No real pod is reserved, no payment is taken and no email is sent. Demo reservations are stored only in this browser.

How does QR access work?+

After booking, your demo pass contains a real, scannable QR code with a StudyBox demo booking identifier. Use “Simulate QR scan” to see the unlock experience. A live service would require secure access hardware and a backend.

Can I change or cancel a session?+

You can cancel a demo reservation from My bookings, then choose a new time. Cancelled slots become available again. Your reservations stay in this browser until you cancel them or clear site storage.

Are these universities StudyBox partners?+

The map uses real university locations in Warsaw to illustrate campus discovery. The pod locations, availability and prices are demo data. No partnerships, installed pods or accessibility certifications are claimed.

Can I book with a friend?+

The current booking demo is for one-person focus sessions. Each booking reserves one whole pod for the selected time. Choose a duration from 30 minutes to 2 hours.

A LITTLE TRANSPARENCY

Study project disclaimer.

StudyBox is a student study project created solely for educational and demonstration purposes. It is a fictional startup concept and is not a live commercial service.

No real study pods can be reserved or unlocked through this website, no payments are processed and no booking confirmations are sent. Product visuals, pod installations, availability and prices are illustrative.

University names and real map locations are used only to demonstrate the concept. This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by or partnered with any university or other organisation shown on the website.

Reservations and the name you provide are stored in your browser’s local storage. They are not sent to StudyBox, and no account, payment or email service is connected. Avoid entering sensitive information on a shared device.

The campus finder embeds Google Maps, which makes a request to Google and is subject to Google’s privacy policy. All other assets are hosted with this site.
